# Hi, I'm Sharad

**I'm a product manager (PM) who likes to explore, learn by building, and make everyday things a little more useful.**

This GitHub is my workshop: a collection of projects I'm exploring to make my work more effective and my personal hobbies more rewarding. Some start with a small frustration. Others start with a question: *Could this be easier, clearer, or more fun?*

You don't need to be technical to look around. Here's what I'm exploring and why.

## Start here

| Project | What it's about | The question behind it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[RunPlan](https://github.com/sharadmangalick/fitness-weekly-email)** | Personalized running plans delivered by email, using activity and recovery information from Garmin or Strava. | Can a training plan fit my goals and how I'm actually feeling? |
| **[DrumBuddy](https://github.com/sharadmangalick/DrumBuddy)** | An iPhone and iPad app that helps kids practice drum rhythms with encouraging feedback. | Can learning to drum feel more like playing? |
| **[Daily Energy Briefing](https://github.com/sharadmangalick/daily-energy-briefing)** | A daily guide that uses Garmin information about sleep, stress, and recovery to help plan the day. | How should I spend my energy today, at work and beyond? |
| **[Garmin Health Analyzer](https://github.com/sharadmangalick/garmin-health-analyzer)** | Reports that make patterns in sleep, fitness, and recovery easier to understand. | What can I learn from the information my watch already collects? |

**Want to see RunPlan outside GitHub?** [Visit the web app](https://fitness-weekly-email.vercel.app).

## What connects these projects

At work, I enjoy making sense of feedback and turning it into clearer product decisions. Outside work, I explore ideas around running, everyday energy, and learning through play.

I use AI tools to help turn ideas into working experiments. The code is a means to an end: understanding a problem, trying something useful, and learning what to improve.

## A workshop, not a finished-product catalog

These projects are at different stages. Some are usable apps; others are prototypes or earlier experiments. They're here to share what I'm exploring, not to suggest everything is polished or ready for everyone.

If you're just browsing, start with a project's introduction. The installation steps and technical details are for people who want to dig deeper.
